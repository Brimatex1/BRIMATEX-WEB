Privacy Policy

Brimatex — Foam & Mattress Manufacturing, Tripoli, Libya

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This policy explains how Brimatex ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you contact us through our Facebook Page, Messenger, WhatsApp, or our lead forms on Facebook and Instagram.

1. Information we collect

When you message us on Messenger or WhatsApp

  • Your public profile name as shown on your Meta account
  • A page-scoped identifier (PSID) issued by Meta, or your WhatsApp number
  • The content of the messages you send us, including any files you attach

When you submit one of our lead forms

  • The name, phone number and business details you enter in the form
  • Technical identifiers supplied by Meta that link the enquiry to the advert

We do not collect payment card details, government identification numbers, or any special category data through these channels.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and provide customer support
  • To prepare quotations, orders and delivery arrangements
  • To assess distributor and wholesale partnership applications
  • To measure how effective our advertising is, in aggregate

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for the independent marketing purposes of any other company.

3. Where it is stored

Messages and enquiries are stored in our Odoo business management system, which is hosted by Odoo S.A. on servers in the European Union. Supporting software runs on hosting provided by Libyan Spider. Access is limited to Brimatex employees who need it for their work, and every account is individually authenticated.

4. Sharing with others

We share personal information only with:

  • Meta Platforms — inherent to using Messenger, WhatsApp and advertising; governed by Meta's own privacy policy
  • Odoo S.A. — as our data processor for business records
  • Our hosting provider — for the technical operation of our systems
  • Authorities — where we are legally obliged to do so

5. How long we keep it

Conversations and enquiries are retained for up to 24 months from the last contact. Where an enquiry becomes a commercial relationship, the related records are kept for as long as required by applicable accounting and tax law.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Write to info@brimatex.ly and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access credentials and API tokens are stored outside publicly reachable directories, and incoming requests from Meta are cryptographically verified before being accepted.

8. Children

Our services are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top will always show the current version. Questions go to:

Brimatex — Al-Andalus District, Tripoli, Libya
info@brimatex.ly
